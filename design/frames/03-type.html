Numbers are mono

Bar, beat, BPM, time, counts. Always tabular so the clock never jitters as digits change.

Caps are mono

Uppercase lives only in mono labels and Archivo display. Sans text is sentence case.

Lyrics are lowercase as sung

The transcript keeps the engine’s case. The Pad title may be caps; the lines stay as sung.

No webfonts in the DAW

WKWebView in Logic loads nothing remote. Anything branded inside the plugin is SVG.

Display is a poster

Archivo appears once or twice per screen: a headline, a section title. Never for UI controls.

Say what happens

Buttons name the action: Apply, Send code, Return to live, Copy position. Status copy says what the engine is doing.
