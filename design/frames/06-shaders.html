Paper Shaders, on brand

Paper’s own WebGL shaders (Apache-2.0, v0.0.81), mounted without React from one vendored file, design/shaders/p2p-shaders.js: 111 KB, 44 KB gzipped, no network. Every color comes from the tokens. Two rules: one moving shader per screen, and nothing moves in the plugin while the music does.
