Motion

Timed to the music where it can be. Everything stops under prefers-reduced-motion except the plate following the playhead, which is the product.

120 ms--t-fast

Hover color and border, word state changes.

200 ms--t-base

Sheet and popover rise 6 px and fade in. Seek wash on the clicked word.

320 ms--t-slow

One synced-scroll step toward the focal line, eased out.

750 ms--t-hold

The plate holds on the last sung word after the final timestamp. Between words it holds with no timeout.

1.3 s--t-pulse

REC dot ring. Banner and stream cursor blink in two steps.

40 · 60%--focal-play · --focal-rec

Where the title line sits in the pane: 40% down in playback, 60% while recording so new words have room below.

Layout

The plugin is a fixed stack around a flexible pane. The site sits on a 12-column grid, 1120 max, 24 gutters, 16 px side gutter on phones.
