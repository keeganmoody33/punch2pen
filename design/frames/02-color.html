Rules

Red is REC

Recording badge, header underline, stream cursor, the R key hint. Nothing else. A struck-out word is grey. The index card’s red header rule is decoration on paper and never a control.

Green is live

Accent, primary action, PLAY and LIVE, corrected and synced. One primary green button per surface.

Amber is waiting

WAIT, the engine banner, pending sync, proposed tags on these boards.

Ink is the plate

The word under the playhead is an ink plate on graphite, never a color fill, so the highlight survives any screen and any color vision.
