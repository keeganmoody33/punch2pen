Rules

One color, one job. The plugin lives inside a DAW, where red already means record, so it spends red more carefully than the site does.

REC is recording

#FF453A colors the recording state and nothing else: the REC badge and its pulsing dot, the header line while recording, the stream cursor, the R key hint. Brighter and more orange than the brand red so the two never read as one.

Red is the pen

On the site: the brand fill with white type, one primary red button per surface. In the plugin: only the red pen, a correction being made or made, and the caret. No red buttons or toggles in a DAW.

White is live

PLAY, LIVE, synced, the playhead, focus rings, the plugin’s primary button. Neutral on purpose, so it can sit next to REC without competing.

Yellow is the highlighter

Legal yellow laid over words: the seek flash after a click moves the playhead, dictionary words, selection. At full strength it is the pad itself.

Amber is waiting

WAIT, the engine banner, pending sync, proposed tags on these boards.

Ink is the plate

The word under the playhead is an ink plate on graphite, never a color fill, so the highlight survives any screen and any color vision.
