Dictionary

Every correction becomes an entry: what the engine heard, what you meant, how often. Adlibs and phrases are first-class, so your “(skrrt)” stops coming back as “skirt”. Signed in, it trains the seat’s profile; free, the same list is session-only and says so in the status bar.

Pen it

The whole reason the product exists: the take leaves the booth written down. Copy and files work on the free tier because they stay on the Mac. A share link needs the profile API, so it follows sign-in.

The lyric sheet

What “Save lyric sheet” produces, on the Pad surface from the index-card sketch. Bar and time sit in the left gutter so an engineer or manager can find any line in the session.

Rules
  • 1
    The gutter carries the clockBar number and m:ss.s in a left gutter, mono, so any line can be found in the session.

    Lyrics sit on the blue rulesSans, 19 px, marker black. One bar per line, as sung.
