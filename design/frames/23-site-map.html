The funnel goes dark at the download click

The plugin, engine, and profile API send no analytics today. Stages 05–08 are the activation story (install, first punch, first correction, sign-in), and they are exactly the ones we cannot see. Any instrumentation there needs an opt-in the free tier can keep honest, since the site promises nothing leaves the Mac on free.
