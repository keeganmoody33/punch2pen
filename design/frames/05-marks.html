Explorations Proposed

Marks drawn from how the plugin works, not adopted. Each could be a sub-brand, a loader, a merch line, or a feature’s icon. Generated by the same script into brand/explore/.

Punch range · [2]

The badge between a DAW’s punch-in and punch-out brackets: the take the pen is writing. It also reads as lyric-sheet notation, [Verse 2] or a bracketed adlib. Fits the transport, the record flow, a “Punch-in” feature name.

Transient

A waveform overview where the bolt is the transient: quiet before, the punch, then decay. The logo’s bolt, read as audio. Works as a loader (bars animate, bolt lands) and ties the fist to what the engine hears.

Seek word

A word under the yellow highlighter with the playhead through it and the timecode on the flag. The product’s unlock in one picture: click a word, move the playhead. A natural icon for the ARA seek feature and for launch posts.
