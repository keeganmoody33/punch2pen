For studios

A seat per artist. The words stay theirs.

Studio is the planned workspace of artist seats for a room that records more than one person. Each seat is one portable dictionary. The artist signed in on that seat is the only one who can read it or train it. Studio and paid sign-in are not available yet.

Isolation

The owner runs the room. The owner does not get the vocal.

What a seat is

An email plus a display name. The artist signs in with that email inside the plugin. Corrections they apply write that seat’s dictionary and follow that login. The next artist on the same Mac does not inherit it.

What the owner can do

Add a seat, or revoke one. Revoking marks the seat revoked and keeps the dictionary rows. The owner does not see the pairs and does not train them. An operator can suspend the workspace; those seats drop the plugin back to the free local session.

That split is enforced in the profile API, not by a promise on this page. The seat dashboard that does the adding and revoking ships with the API. It is not hosted on this website.

The room

Same plugin. Separate dictionaries.

  • A commercial room can put a seat on each artist who punches in, so yesterday’s names and ad-libs do not bias today’s vocal.
  • A&R can hand a seat to the artist and still not hold their dictionary.
  • The clock stays in the plugin, locked to the DAW in front of them. The seat stores correction pairs, not the timeline.
  • Logic on Mac is the host we run. This page does not add a Windows room, an iOS booth, or another DAW.
